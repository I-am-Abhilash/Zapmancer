Absolutely. For **Zapmancer**, I would make the web-only home screen feel like a **premium global freelancing marketplace**, rather than simply making the mobile app's first screen responsive.

The key idea is:

> **Mobile app = users come to do work.**
> **Web landing page = visitors come to understand Zapmancer, trust it, and choose what they want to do.**

So the landing page should be a marketing experience with a clear path for both sides of your marketplace: **clients who need talent** and **freelancers who want work**.

## The most important design decision I'd make

I would **not make the page look like a traditional corporate SaaS website** with lots of generic cards and tiny text.

Zapmancer should have a strong visual identity:

> **Premium + modern + human + technology-driven**

Think of the experience as a combination of:

**Marketplace** + **Professional network** + **Modern SaaS product**

The hero should immediately answer:

> **"What is Zapmancer?"**

The two-path section should answer:

> **"What can I do here?"**

The Products section should answer:

> **"What can Zapmancer provide?"**

The Solutions section should answer:

> **"Can Zapmancer solve my specific problem?"**

The Resources section should answer:

> **"Can I learn enough to make a good decision?"**

And the final CTA should answer:

> **"What do I do next?"**

That gives you a **very coherent landing page architecture** rather than simply copying the common "Products / Solutions / Resources" labels.

For your **KMP web implementation**, I would also keep this landing page completely separate from the authenticated application shell. In other words, `/` is your **marketing landing page**, while `/app` is your actual **Zapmancer marketplace application**. On mobile, the user can go directly into the application experience, while on desktop/web you can have this rich landing page before authentication.
